### Five new tools

**Mic Control** — mute your input everywhere at once.
**Snippets** — a searchable library of reusable text.
**Drive Ejector** — eject external volumes safely.
**Scratchpad** — an always-there notepad.
**Network Info** — your IP, router and DNS at a glance.

### Improvements

- Download Video: audio-only downloads (M4A/MP3)
- Clipboard History: pull text out of images with OCR
- QR Studio: Wi-Fi/contact/email templates, and decoding from an image
- Calendar: hide individual calendars, click a day to open it
- Dev Tools: JWT decoder
- ⌘C/⌘V/⌘Z now work in every tool's text fields

### Fixed

62 bugs from a full code review, settings panels that overflowed on some
displays, and a number of Volume Mixer and Audio Switcher issues.
