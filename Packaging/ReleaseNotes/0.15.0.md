### New tool

**Video Splitter** — drop in a long video, get back clips that fit a 15, 30, 60
or 90 second upload cap. Each video gets its own clips folder, named so they sort
in playback order.

- Cuts several clips at once: four at a time on a 16‑core Mac (26s → 19s on a
  1080p30 test source), and a single clip still gets the whole machine
- Choose the **CPU** encoder (x264 — quickest on a many‑core Mac) or the **GPU**
  one (VideoToolbox — encodes on the media engine, so the CPU and the battery
  are left alone)
- Clips are re‑encoded, not stream‑copied, so every one lands just under the cap
  instead of overshooting to the next keyframe
- Cancel mid‑run at any time; nothing half‑written is left behind

Adds nothing to the download — it uses the ffmpeg already shipped with Download
Video.
