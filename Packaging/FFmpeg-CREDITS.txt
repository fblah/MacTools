FFmpeg
======

DMonte Video Downloader bundles prebuilt static FFmpeg binaries (ffmpeg and
ffprobe) in Contents/Resources/bin. yt-dlp invokes them as a separate child
process to merge separate video/audio streams and to recode to MP4.

Platform:  macOS arm64, statically linked (no external dependencies)
